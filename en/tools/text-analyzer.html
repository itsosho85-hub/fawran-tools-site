How is reading time calculated?

The tool uses a standard average reading speed (roughly 200 words per minute) to estimate how long it takes to read the full text — useful before publishing an article or post to know how long it will feel to a reader.

How is complexity determined?

The tool calculates average word length and average number of words per sentence. Text with consistently long sentences or complex words gets classified as "Complex," while short, direct text gets classified as "Easy."

Privacy

All analysis happens locally in your browser using fixed algorithms — your text is never sent to any external server.
