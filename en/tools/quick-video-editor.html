How to use the quick editor

  1. Click the box and choose one or more videos — you can add photos to the same strip too.
  2. Pick output quality and aspect ratio (16:9 for platforms, 9:16 for reels and shorts), then open the editor.
  3. Drag to reorder clips, or delete anything you don't want.
  4. Add music or a voice-over if you'd like, then click "Export video" and wait for it to finish.

Privacy

Your videos or photos are never uploaded to any server; all trimming, merging, and export happens locally in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Are my videos uploaded to any server?

No, all processing happens locally in your browser from start to finish — files never leave your device.

How many videos can I merge?

There's no fixed limit, but very large files may slow performance depending on your device.

Can I add photos between clips?

Yes, you can mix videos and photos on the same strip in any order you want.

What format is the output video?

Export is in WebM format, supported by every modern browser and most publishing platforms.
