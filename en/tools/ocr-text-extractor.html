What kinds of images work best?

The tool works well with clear, high-contrast images: screenshots, scanned documents, book pages, or photos with clean, unrotated text. Accuracy drops with tilted images, handwriting, or very low-quality photos.

Privacy

Your image is never uploaded to any server at any stage — text recognition happens entirely in your browser via WebAssembly technology. The only thing downloaded from the internet is the recognition engine itself (generic files shared across all users, unrelated to your image's content).
